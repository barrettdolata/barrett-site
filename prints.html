How it works

  1. You subscribe

    Sign up for $6 a month and give me the address you want it mailed to.

  2. I pick a piece

    Each month I choose something new from my prints, paintings and drawings and print it as a postcard. Sometimes there’s a sticker too.

  3. It shows up

    Your card is mailed in an envelope the last week of the month, so you can keep it or pass it along with a note of your own. What’s inside stays a secret until it’s in your mailbox.
