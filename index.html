Writer, reporter & artist

I’m a journalist from Oxford, Michigan, who chases any story and brings an artist’s sensibility to whatever I cover. I’m not afraid of hard subjects, and I handle them with care. In my reporting, my art and my poems, I’m drawn to contrast, the humor and the grief, the beauty and the darkness, the strange little details that let two things be true at once.

I interned on the features desk at The Detroit News in summer 2025 and was asked to stay on as a freelancer. I still write features on arts, culture and local business for the paper and am looking for full-time work in New York City. I was previously business senior news editor at The Michigan Daily and have more than three years of experience reporting features, hard news and community stories, from daily breaking news to long-form enterprise pieces. I also co-host Off-Beat, a reported audio podcast, with Emma Spring.

I graduated from the University of Michigan in 2026 with a bachelor’s degree in honors English and a minor in art and design. My studio work includes drawing, painting, collage, ceramics and intaglio and relief printmaking, and my poetry draws on family, humor and everyday life.
